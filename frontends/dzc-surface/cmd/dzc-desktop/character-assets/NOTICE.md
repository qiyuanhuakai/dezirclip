# Character preview artwork and fonts

Flag artwork by the Twemoji contributors and Twitter, Inc., used under
[CC BY 4.0](TWEMOJI-LICENSE.txt). DezirClip resizes the artwork and packs it
into an image atlas.
[Original Twemoji artwork](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji/tree/b6b55fef1e8636b540a6d016a4729ca8cdf2e60b/assets/72x72).

The DezirClip preview fonts are renamed subsets of Noto Sans Symbols 2,
Noto Music, Noto Sans Math, and Noto Sans. They are distributed under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1. See the accompanying OFL files for the original
copyright notices and license.
